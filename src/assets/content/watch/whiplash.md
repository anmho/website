**Takeaway to revisit:** Greatness demands sacrifice. Decide how badly you want it, what you’re willing to give up, and whether the ambition is truly yours. Balance is not obligatory, but neither is every sacrifice justified.

**Related tenets:** [Choose your values before others choose them for you](/tenets#choose-values-before-others-choose-them); [ask your future self before sacrificing the present](/tenets#ask-your-future-self-before-sacrificing-the-present).

**On a rewatch, ask:** Do I want the achievement enough to choose the life it requires, or do I only want the result?

This is a reflection on ambition and its costs, not a claim that abuse is necessary for excellence or that sacrifice guarantees greatness.
