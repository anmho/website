**Takeaway to revisit:** Sometimes becoming the people you each want to be means you are not together in that reality. That does not make the love unreal or the relationship a failure. You can cherish what someone helped you become and grieve the future you did not share.

**Reminder:** A meaningful choice can still leave you mourning another meaningful life. Sadness alone does not prove you chose wrong.

**Related tenet:** [Choose deliberately. Commit without dwelling.](/tenets).

**On a rewatch, ask:** Can I honor the life we might have had without treating the life I chose as a mistake?

This is one interpretation to revisit, not a rule that love and ambition must be incompatible.
