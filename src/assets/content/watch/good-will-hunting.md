**Takeaway to revisit:** Self-protection can keep you from relationships and possibilities you genuinely value. Being capable does not mean you must choose the most prestigious path.

**Related tenet:** [Making hard decisions is impossible without knowing your values](/tenets#choose-the-life-not-just-the-metric).

**On a rewatch, ask:** Am I saying no because this path conflicts with my values, or because being known and taking a chance feels frightening?

This is a reflection prompt, not a claim that the film has one definitive lesson.
