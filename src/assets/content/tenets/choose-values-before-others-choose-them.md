If you do not examine and choose your values, other people and institutions will supply them for you. This is a paraphrase of Nietzsche's warning: "Whatever cannot obey itself, is commanded."

Source: [Friedrich Nietzsche, *Thus Spake Zarathustra*, Part II, “Self-Surpassing” (trans. Thomas Common)](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/1998/1998-h/1998-h.htm)
