When in doubt, ask yourself: if your life were a movie, what would the main character do?

This is inspired by the [“Space Oddity” helicopter scene in *The Secret Life of Walter Mitty*](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HEwtPwkeXjw), where Walter moves from imagining an adventure to taking part in one. The question is not an invitation to perform for an audience; it is a prompt to choose the vivid, courageous, and meaningful path when the safer default is merely passive.
