When in doubt, ask yourself: if your life were a movie, what would the main character do?

This is inspired by [*The Secret Life of Walter Mitty*](https://www.20thcenturystudios.com/movies/the-secret-life-of-walter-mitty), a story about moving from imagined adventure into an intentional life. The question is not an invitation to perform for an audience; it is a prompt to choose the vivid, courageous, and meaningful path when the safer default is merely passive.
