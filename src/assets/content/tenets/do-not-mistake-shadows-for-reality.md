Familiar appearances and expectations can feel like the whole world when they are all you have known. Growth requires the uncomfortable turn toward clearer understanding rather than mistaking shadows for reality.

Source: [Plato, *Republic*, Book VII, 514a-518d](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0168%3Abook%3D7%3Asection%3D514a)
