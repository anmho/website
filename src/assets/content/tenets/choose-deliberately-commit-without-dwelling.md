> “Life’s simple, you make choices and you don’t look back.”

Han in *The Fast and the Furious: Tokyo Drift* (2006). [Watch the clip](https://clip.cafe/the-fast-the-furious-tokyo-drift-2006/lifes-simple-s1/).

Give your choice a real chance instead of constantly romanticizing the path you did not take. Look back to learn, not to punish yourself. Change course when the evidence or your values change, not merely because commitment feels uncomfortable.
