When comparing paths, consider how each might affect your well-being, not just its pay, prestige, or upside.

[Seligman’s PERMA model](https://positivepsychology.com/perma-model/), a well-being framework rooted in positive psychology, offers prompts: positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment. Use these to understand what a choice could give you and cost you, not as requirements to satisfy equally.

Happiness need not outweigh every other priority. The point is to make that tradeoff consciously.

Ask: “Beyond what I would achieve, what would living this choice feel like?”
