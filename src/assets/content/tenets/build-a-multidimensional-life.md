Make room for positive emotion, deep engagement, strong relationships, meaning, and accomplishment. A good life should not be reduced to a single metric.

Inspired by [Seligman’s PERMA model](https://positivepsychology.com/perma-model/), a framework that describes wellbeing through positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment. Use it as a check on what you are building, not as a scorecard that turns living into optimization.
