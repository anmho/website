A higher salary, greater upside, or more prestigious title can be a valid reason to choose a path, but examine what that path actually asks of you and offers you. Use frameworks to uncover considerations you might otherwise miss, then weigh them according to your own values.

The goal is not a balanced scorecard. It is a decision whose reasoning you understand and endorse, including the sacrifices. One priority may dominate; make sure you know why.

Ask: “Why is this the right choice for me, rather than merely an impressive choice?”

[Seligman’s PERMA model](https://positivepsychology.com/perma-model/) is a framework for well-being, not a general decision-making framework. It describes positive emotion, engagement, relationships, meaning, and accomplishment.

Use it here to ask: “Beyond pay or upside, how might this choice affect my enjoyment, engagement, relationships, sense of meaning, and accomplishment?”

These dimensions help you examine the life a choice could create. Your values determine their importance; the framework does not choose for you or require equal weight across them. Applying PERMA this way is a personal reflection practice, not a decision rule supplied by the model.
