When uncertain, favor the choice that teaches you more, especially when you can change course. If you are torn about moving to a new city and can afford to move back, go. Experience can answer what speculation cannot.

Reversible does not mean costless. Know what you can recover, what you cannot, and whether the lesson is worth it.

Source: The reversibility distinction draws on [Jeff Bezos's 2015 shareholder letter](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/files/doc_financials/annual/2015-Letter-to-Shareholders.PDF). Favoring information gain is my own heuristic.
