Make time alone to hear yourself. Notice when you are drifting into the status games of your city, industry, or friend group, adopting their goals, ambitions, and measures of success without deciding whether they matter to you.

Step back regularly and ask: “Do I want this, or do I want to be admired by the people who want this?”

Solitude is not about rejecting influence. It is about deciding which influences deserve to become part of you. Choose which games deserve your life; you do not have to win every game around you.
