You can recognize how much you have changed and still underestimate how much you will change again. Beware of romanticizing one version of your life as the life you will always want.

Choose a direction without organizing everything around reaching and preserving that imagined destination. Leave room to outgrow it.

Source: [Ben Felix, *Using Your Money To Be Happier*, around 9:20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S9InNdQhFwc&t=560s); [Quoidbach, Gilbert, and Wilson, “The End of History Illusion” (2013)](https://midus.wisc.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/1256.pdf).
