Primal instincts and inherited values—from family, wealth, capitalism, individualism, and materialism—will shape you; neither suppress them nor let them dominate you. Build personal values that can affirm or override them; keep them stable enough to guide hard decisions, revise them through lived experience, conversation, and reflection, and treat unstable decision-making as a prompt to clarify what you value.
