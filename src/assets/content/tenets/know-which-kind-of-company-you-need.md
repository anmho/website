Some friends help you enjoy the present; others help you build toward the future. Appreciate what each friendship brings without expecting one person to meet every need.

Notice what you need in the moment, and seek that company intentionally. Leave room for friendships to grow beyond the roles you associate with them.

Alignment is the goal, not balance. Neither orientation deserves equal weight by default. Your values may lead you to prioritize one far more than the other, and sometimes you will have to choose.

Be wary of spending so much time around one kind of company that their priorities quietly replace your own. Let your values determine which takes priority, and notice when the people around you start making that choice for you.
