Some friends help you enjoy the present; others help you build toward the future. Appreciate what each friendship brings without expecting one person to meet every need.

Notice what you need in the moment, and seek that company intentionally. Leave room for friendships to grow beyond the roles you associate with them.

Be mindful of spending too much time at either extreme: living only for the present can leave your future unattended, while living only for the future can mean postponing life itself. Seek a balance that lets you enjoy your life and build it.
