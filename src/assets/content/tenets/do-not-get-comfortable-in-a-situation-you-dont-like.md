Do not mistake adaptation for alignment. If a situation consistently makes you unhappy, do not let familiarity turn it into a life you defend. Ask whether you genuinely chose the tradeoff or simply learned to want what feels available.

This is a personal extension of [Jon Elster's concept of adaptive preferences](https://www.cambridge.org/core/books/abs/utilitarianism-and-beyond/sour-grapes-utilitarianism-and-the-genesis-of-wants/A0E4BFE0600CE2C6DD5E61B335969AB7): preferences can be shaped by perceived constraints. Adaptation is not always bad, and acceptance can be wise; the tenet is a prompt to check whether your comfort is chosen or merely acclimated.
