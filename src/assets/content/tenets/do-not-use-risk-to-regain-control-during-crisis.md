When hunger, lust, exhaustion, sadness, or depression is driving an impulse, pause before an irreversible or high-impact choice. If your values are unclear, clarify them before committing.

Address the immediate need and revisit the decision when you can think more clearly. Do not use risk to regain a sense of control. The aim is deliberation, not waiting for a perfect mood.

Source: A personal extension of the HALT reminder (Hungry, Angry, Lonely, Tired), used in the [VA's anger-management resources](https://www.veterantraining.va.gov/apps/aims/resources/documents/CommonAngerTriggers.pdf), not a rule that every emotion invalidates judgment.
