Seek new people, ideas, stories, and experiences that can challenge, reinforce, or update your worldview. Let your beliefs remain flexible, but let your core values provide a stable basis for action.

Sources: [Julia Galef, *The Scout Mindset*](https://www.penguinrandomhouse.com/books/555240/the-scout-mindset-by-julia-galef/); [American Psychological Association, Acceptance and Commitment Therapy](https://www.apa.org/pubs/books/acceptance-commitment-therapy).
