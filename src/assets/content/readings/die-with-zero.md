*Die With Zero* resonates because it treats time as the scarce resource underneath money. Saving can create freedom, but postponing every experience until some imagined future can turn prudence into missed life.

The book's useful ideas include an experiences curve, time buckets, and memory dividends. I read them as prompts to match spending and ambition to the seasons of life when they can actually be enjoyed, while keeping enough margin to avoid reckless decisions.
