In Book VII of [Plato's *Republic*](https://www.perseus.tufts.edu/hopper/text?doc=Perseus%3Atext%3A1999.01.0168%3Abook%3D7%3Asection%3D514a), the allegory of the cave gives the underlying language for what *The Truman Show* makes visceral: a person can mistake a constructed world for reality when it is all they have known. The turn toward truth is painful, and clearer vision brings a responsibility to choose how to live once the shadows stop being enough.
